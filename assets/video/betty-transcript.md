# Tested with Betty: transcript

**Video:** `betty.mp4`, 58 seconds. **Subtitles:** `betty.en.vtt`.

Betty is totally blind and uses VoiceOver on her iPhone every day. On 15 November 2026 she tried an early version of Earth Jukebox on her own iPhone. Our team (Kevin and Alvin) guided her over a Zoom call. The clip uses the real Zoom audio. The screen in the clip is our computer following her steps, not her phone. Her photo is from the same day, from the end of the call, when she turned her camera on.

The clip has five short parts, in the order they happened. We cut out the quiet stretches while she was finding buttons, and one part (5) is from a follow-up call later that morning.

She was speaking Mandarin, except where marked. The English translation was made with AI assistance (Anthropic Claude) and checked against the recording. Words in [brackets] could not be heard clearly.
